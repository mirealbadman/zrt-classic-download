WILL MY SAVE DATA CARRY OVER?+

Usually, updating an app with the same package name and signing certificate preserves local data. Don't uninstall your existing build first. If Android rejects the update, stop before removing anything.

CAN I PLAY WITHOUT A CONTROLLER?+

Yes. The original touchscreen controls remain available. Supported gamepads provide an additional way to play during gameplay.

WHY DOES ANDROID ASK FOR PERMISSION?+

This is an APK downloaded outside the Play Store. Android may ask you to allow installation from your browser or Files app. Only install files you trust, and turn off that permission again afterward if you prefer.

No. This is an independent, unofficial compatibility effort. Zombie Road Trip and its original content belong to their respective rights holders.
